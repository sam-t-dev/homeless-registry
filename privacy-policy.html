Privacy Policy

Anonymous Homeless Registry · Effective 26 September 2026

Summary

We believe everyone has the right to privacy, especially those experiencing homelessness. This policy explains what this site collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

We collect zero personally identifying information. Recorded fields are only:

  • Approximate city (text you provide)
  • Sleeping situation category
  • Rough duration
  • Optional needs tags from a fixed list
  • Timestamp
  • One-time anonymous token for self-deletion

We do not collect names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, GPS, photos, free-text narratives, IP logs, or browser fingerprints.

How information is used

  • Public display shows aggregate counts only
  • Your token is the only handle for deleting your record
  • Current live demo stores submissions in your browser (localStorage). A shared GitHub-backed pipeline remains work-in-progress

Deletion, retention, security

Use your token link to delete. Records remain until you delete them. No PII is designed into the schema, limiting re-identification risk from aggregates.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the GitHub repository.
