Privacy Policy

Anonymous Homeless Registry · Effective 26 September 2026

Summary

We believe everyone has the right to privacy, especially those experiencing homelessness. This policy explains what this site collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

We collect zero personally identifying information. Recorded fields are only country, approximate city, sleeping situation, duration, optional needs tags, timestamp, and an anonymous token.

We do not collect names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, GPS, photos, free-text narratives, IP logs, or browser fingerprints.

How information is used

  • Public display shows aggregate counts only
  • Your token is the only handle for deleting your record
  • Current live demo stores submissions in your browser (localStorage)
