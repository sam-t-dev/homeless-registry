# Privacy Policy - Anonymous Homeless Registry ## Effective Date: [Current Date] ## Summary We believe that everyone has the right to privacy, especially those experiencing homelessness. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information on this website. ## Information We Collect **We collect ZERO personally identifying information.** The only data we record is: - Approximate city (text you provide, no street addresses or exact locations) - Sleeping situation category (rough sleeping, vehicle, couch surfing, other) - Rough duration (time period you select) - Optional basic needs tags (food, shelter, medical - selected from predefined list) - Timestamp of submission - One-time anonymous token (UUID-generated, allows you to delete your own record) We do NOT collect: - Names, nicknames, or aliases - Phone numbers or contact information - Email addresses - Physical addresses or GPS coordinates - Photos or images - Free-text descriptions or narratives - IP addresses (we do not log them) - Browser fingerprinting data ## How We Use Information - Records are stored in aggregated form only - Public display shows counts by city and situation only - no individual records are visible - Your anonymous token is the only way to identify and manage your specific record - Aggregated counts are updated automatically every 5 minutes ## Data Deletion - Each submission receives a unique anonymous token - You can use this token to delete your own record at any time - Deletion is performed through a secure, token-verified process - Once deleted, the record is removed from all counts immediately ## Data Retention - Records remain in the system until explicitly deleted by the submitter - No automatic expiration or deletion schedule - You control how long your record stays active ## Security - No encryption required since no sensitive personal data is collected - All data is stored in a public GitHub repository - The system is designed so no individual can be re-identified from aggregated data - Tokens are one-time use only, preventing reuse or tracking ## Changes to This Policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating this page and modifying the effective date. ## Contact If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the repository issues. --- *Last updated: [Date]*
